Conclusion
 Gravity has significant effects on the material's flammability.
o Nomex HT90-40  behaves differently than other materials when burned.
 Oxygen concentration is observed to increase flame length and tip spread rate.
o Inconclusive for base spread rate (need more testing).
 Flow rate has negligible effect on flame length and flame spread over the tested range.
 Likely due to the buoyant force outweighing the imposed flow
* Assuming a buoyant velocity of 25 cm/s
